Pick the single emoji that most makes a stranger want to book a dream
vacation, judged as a wordless postcard: it must plainly name a dream
vacation, evoke the trip vividly at a glance, feel personal and specific
rather than generic, carry a spark of wit or surprise, and read instantly
across cultures and screens.

My pick: the desert island (U+1F3DD) — the classic escape fantasy, a lone
palm island in open sea. Write it with no variation selector so the entry
is exactly one code point (1 character, 4 bytes UTF-8), keeping the file
minimal for the byte-count tie-break.
